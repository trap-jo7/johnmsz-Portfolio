# Portfolio design and content policy

Approved by John on November 17, 2026.

## Brand

- White `#FFFFFF`: primary canvas.
- Mist `#F3F5F7`: experience section background.
- Navy `#172536`: headings and primary text.
- Slate `#526171`: supporting text.
- Blue `#245A81`: links, primary actions, evidence rules.
- Divider gray `#D8E0E8`: section separators.

Source Serif 4 is reserved for the name and section headings. Source Sans 3 is used for navigation, body copy, dates, and controls. Voice: direct, factual, and specific to documented experience. The signature element is a thin blue rule beside project evidence and education.

## Section patterns

- Introduction: name-led, with degree and project summary. A compact education sidebar provides immediate recruiter context.
- Selected projects: three resume-backed project rows. Each separates project scope, technologies, contribution, and linked evidence without mock screenshots or simulated telemetry.
- More on GitHub: a small secondary repository list, so ongoing experiments do not receive equal emphasis to resume projects.
- Experience: a chronological record of documented roles, dates, organizations, and responsibilities.
- Technical foundation: a plain list of resume-listed skills, with no proficiency ratings.
- Contact: direct email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and the actual downloadable resume.

References reviewed: https://jvns.ca/, https://sive.rs/, https://swyx.io/. The shared pattern is a clear identity, content hierarchy, and direct links to real work; no personal claims are taken from those sites.

## Motion

One 700ms, four-pixel introduction entrance with no opacity change; content is always visible. Button feedback uses 180ms ease-out transitions. Reduced motion disables both. Native navigation and links need no JavaScript.

## Evidence rules

The source resume is `public/john-soliz-resume.pdf`. Use it for education, experience, skills, languages, and the three selected projects. LinkedIn URL is carried forward from the original portfolio. Additional repository descriptions come from their GitHub READMEs.

NetScope's `docs/interview.md` identifies AI assistance and John's lab operation, testing, evidence interpretation, and recovery validation. The portfolio must preserve that distinction. Its 15-trial result describes a local lab only. EPL Predictor's website includes sample data and is separate from its trained model. Do not claim benchmark accuracy. Gallery uploads persist on the visitor's own device.

No invented work history, certifications, clients, testimonials, production impact, statistics, independent authorship, or proficiency ratings. Avoid exaggeration even when wording is strengthened.
